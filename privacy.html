Privacy Policy

Effective 22 October 2026 · Applies to the Koshyk app, its server and shared-list web pages.

Short version

  • You can use Koshyk without an account. Then your lists stay on your device and in your own iCloud.
  • Our server only receives data if you sign in or share a list.
  • Receipt photos never leave your device.
  • No ads, no data sales, no third-party trackers, no cookies.

Who is responsible (data controller)

Koshyk. Contact: denyshlova@gmail.com.

1. On your device

Lists, items, purchase history, holiday lists, receipts read on the device (store, date, lines, totals), receipt photos, category choices and settings. Receipts are read by on-device text recognition.

The app keeps simple anonymous counts on the device, such as “a list was closed”. They contain no names, products, sums or photos and are not sent anywhere. You can turn them off in Settings. In the UK and EU the app asks before counting.

2. In your iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, the app syncs your lists, history and receipt lines between your devices through your private iCloud database. Apple runs it under Apple's privacy policy. We cannot read it. Receipt photos are not synced.

Legal basis (GDPR): performance of the service you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b)). Retention: until you delete your account. Revoking a share link stops it working immediately.

4. Barcode lookups

When you scan a barcode, the app sends only the barcode number to our server. The server looks it up in Open Food Facts and caches the product by barcode. Your identity and IP address are not sent to Open Food Facts, and the cache is not linked to you.

5. Shared-list web pages

Anyone who has a share link can see that list and tick rows, without signing in. Only share links with people you trust.

A visitor can type a name so others see who ticked a row. The name is optional, is sent to our server only when the visitor ticks a row, and is deleted with the list owner's account.

6. Cookies

We do not use cookies, advertising identifiers or third-party analytics anywhere. The shared-list page keeps the visitor's own typed name in that browser's local storage, so it does not need to be typed again. It never leaves the browser except as described above, and you can clear it in your browser settings. Because this storage is strictly necessary for a feature you use, no cookie banner is required.

7. Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple. We never receive your card details or Apple ID.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR, the Law of Ukraine “On Personal Data Protection”, or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you can access, correct, delete, export or restrict your data, and object to processing.

  • Export: Settings → export your history as a file.
  • Delete: Settings → delete everything, on the phone and on the server. This deletes your account, lists, history and share links, and disconnects Koshyk from your Apple ID.
  • Or email denyshlova@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.

We do not sell or “share” personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising. You can complain to your data protection authority.

9. Children

Koshyk is not directed at children under 13, or under 16 in the EEA. Please do not sign in on behalf of a child.

10. Security

Traffic is encrypted with HTTPS. Session keys are stored as hashes. Share links use long random tokens.

11. Changes

We will update this page and its effective date before any change in how data is handled. For important changes, we will also tell you in the app.

4. Пошук за штрихкодом

Коли ви скануєте штрихкод, застосунок надсилає на наш сервер лише його номер. Сервер шукає товар в Open Food Facts і кешує результат за штрихкодом. Ваша особа та IP-адреса до Open Food Facts не передаються, а кеш не прив'язаний до вас.

5. Вебсторінки спільних списків

Будь-хто, хто має посилання, бачить цей список і може закреслювати товари без входу. Діліться посиланнями лише з тими, кому довіряєте.

Відвідувач може вписати ім'я, щоб інші бачили, хто закреслив товар. Ім'я необов'язкове, надсилається на сервер лише під час закреслення і видаляється разом з акаунтом власника списку.

6. Cookie

Ми ніде не використовуємо cookie, рекламних ідентифікаторів чи сторонньої аналітики. Сторінка спільного списку зберігає вписане відвідувачем ім'я в локальному сховищі його браузера, щоб не вводити його знову. Воно не залишає браузер, крім випадку, описаного вище, і його можна стерти в налаштуваннях браузера. Це сховище суворо необхідне для функції, якою ви користуєтеся, тому банер згоди на cookie не потрібен.
